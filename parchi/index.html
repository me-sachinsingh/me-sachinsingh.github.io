Parchi: Home Bills & Hisaab

पर्ची: घर का हिसाब

A household ledger for Android: milk, newspaper, house help, monthly bills and more. Mark only the days that differ, and send a clear bill at month-end. It works offline, with no account and no ads, in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
