Gemlit privacy policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Publisher: Sachin Singh, individual developer, India. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the publisher.

Contact: skydays.support@gmail.com. That address is shared with our other apps, so please put "Gemlit" in your subject line.

Gemlit is a logic-puzzle game for Android (package app.gemlit.puzzles) with three kinds of puzzle: Stars, Tents and Bridges. It is free and shows ads. It has no account or login, and we run no server for it. We, the publisher, do not receive any data from the app.

What the app itself does with your data

Nothing leaves your phone because of the app itself. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or files, and it has no sign-in.

Your game progress stays on your phone, in the app's own private storage: which puzzles you solved, boards you have not finished, your best times, your settings (sound, vibration and so on), and small counters that limit how often a full-screen ad may appear. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it. We never see it. If Android backup is switched on, Android may include this game data in your own Google account backup and restore it when you reinstall; we never see it.

Ads and Google's software

The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and, now and then, a full-screen ad between puzzles (never during a puzzle or the tutorial). When you run out of hints, you can also choose to watch a short rewarded video ad for more hints; it plays only if you tap to watch it. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show them, the app includes Google's Mobile Ads software and Google's consent software (User Messaging Platform). This software, not us, collects and uses data and sends it to Google and its advertising partners over an encrypted connection. According to Google, this can include:

  • Device and other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and an app set ID.
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address. The app has no location permission and never reads GPS.
  • App interactions, such as when the app is opened and when an ad is shown or tapped.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance information about the ad software.

Google uses this to show ads (including personalised ads where the law and your choices allow), to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google decides how it is used and how long it is kept, under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested (the first time you reach the app's home screen, after the short tutorial). You can change your answer at any time in the app: Settings, Privacy choices. That row appears whenever the form applies to you.
  • In US states with privacy laws, where Google's software offers an opt-out, the same Privacy choices row appears in Settings.
  • On any Android phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in the phone's settings (usually Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • You can stop all of this by uninstalling the app.

The app's Settings, Privacy policy row opens this page.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Used only by Google's ad software, to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (and Android's ad-services permissions). Used only by Google's ad software.
  • Wake lock and foreground service. Declared by a background-work library that Google's ad software uses.

The game itself needs no permission. The app asks for no camera, location, contacts, microphone, phone, SMS or storage permission.

Children

Gemlit is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have a concern, write to us.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. If Android backup is switched on, Android may include this game data in your own Google account backup and restore it when you reinstall; we never see it. Data held by Google is under Google's controls: see My Ad Center and the phone settings above. If you email us, we keep the email only as long as needed to answer you, and delete it on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new effective date.
