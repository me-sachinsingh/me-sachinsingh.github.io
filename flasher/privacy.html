Flasher Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Publisher: Sachin Singh, individual developer, India. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the publisher.

Contact: skydays.support@gmail.com. That address is shared with our other apps, so please put "Flasher" in your subject line.

Flasher is a flashlight app for Android (package com.sgen.flasher). It switches your phone's camera light on and off, and can flash it at a speed you choose. It is free and shows a banner ad. It has no account or login, and we run no server for it. We, the publisher, do not receive any data from the app.

What the app itself does with your data

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or files. It uses the camera's light only; it does not take pictures, record video or look at the camera image, and it asks for no camera permission. It stores nothing about you on the phone apart from what Google's ad software keeps, described below.

Ads and Google's software

The app shows one banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To do that, the app includes Google's Mobile Ads software and Google's consent software (User Messaging Platform). This software, not us, collects and uses data, and sends it to Google and its advertising partners over an encrypted connection. According to Google, this can include:

  • Device and other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and an app set ID.
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address. The app has no location permission and never reads GPS.
  • App interactions, such as when the app is opened and when an ad is shown or tapped.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance information about the ad software.

Google uses this to show ads, including personalised ads where the law and your choices allow, to measure them, and to prevent fraud. Google decides how it is used and kept, under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your answer at any time by tapping Ad privacy choices on the app's screen, which appears when the form applies to you.
  • In US states with privacy laws, where Google's software provides an opt-out, the same Ad privacy choices link appears.
  • On any Android phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (usually Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • You can stop all of this by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Used only by Google's ad software to load the banner.
  • Advertising ID. Used only by Google's ad software.

The flashlight itself needs no permission. The app asks for no camera, location, contacts, microphone, phone, SMS or storage permission.

Children

Flasher is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have a concern, write to us.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google is under Google's controls: see My Ad Center and the phone settings above. If you email us, we keep the email only as long as needed to answer you, and delete it on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new effective date.
